# One AI Hackathon 2025 - SupplyGuard AI

## 🚀 Project Overview

SupplyGuard AI is an AI-driven supply chain disruption predictor developed during the One AI Hackathon 2025. Our solution leverages artificial intelligence to predict potential supply chain disruptions 30-60 days in advance and recommends proactive mitigation strategies.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Modern supply chains face constant threats from supplier delays, transportation issues, demand volatility, and external factors. Traditional planning tools are reactive and lack predictive capabilities. ABC Integrated Supply Chain needs an AI-based system that identifies potential risks before they materialize and recommends corrective actions.

## 💡 Solution: SupplyGuard AI Features

- **Predictive Risk Scoring**: AI-powered analysis of supply chain vulnerabilities
- **Early Warning System**: 30-60 day advance disruption prediction
- **Mitigation Recommendations**: Supplier diversification, inventory rebalancing, logistics rerouting
- **Real-time Monitoring**: Continuous assessment of supply chain health
- **Decision Intelligence**: Actionable insights for supply chain resilience

# One AI Hackathon 2025

## SupplyGuard AI (AI-Driven Supply Chain Disruption Predictor):
### Duration: 10 Hours (One Day) 

#### 1. Challenge Objective
Your mission is to develop an AI-powered supply chain risk intelligence system that predicts disruptions and recommends mitigation strategies across procurement, production, and distribution. Modern supply chains are exposed to disruptions from supplier delays, transportation issues, demand volatility, and external factors. Traditional planning tools are reactive and lack predictive capabilities. Your solution must demonstrate how AI can anticipate risks before they materialize. This challenge requires combining predictive modelling, risk scoring, and decision intelligence -not just forecasting.

#### 2. The Scenario: 
"ABC Integrated Supply Chain" ABC operates a complex, multi-tier supply chain spanning raw material suppliers, manufacturing plants, warehouses, and distribution networks. Even small disruptions in key components can lead to production delays, stock shortages, or cost overruns. ABC leadership wants an Al-based system that identifies potential supply chain risks 30-60 days in advance and recommends corrective actions such as supplier diversification, inventory rebalancing, or logistics rerouting. Your team must design a proof of concept that demonstrates how Al can enhance supply chain resilience and decision-making.
